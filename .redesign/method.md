# forge3d editor visual method

- The near-black canvas is the anchor and the viewport is the product's visual protagonist.
- Separation comes from one-step surface shifts and one-pixel hairlines, not drop shadows.
- Density is compact and tool-like, using a four-pixel base rhythm.
- Global state stays in a slim top bar; object actions stay spatially attached to the viewport.
- The left shelf carries only working tools and gives the active tool a restrained accent fill.
- The right dock is secondary, collapsible context: Transform first, then Conversation and Log.
- Emphasis uses ink weight or one lavender accent; large accent-filled regions are forbidden.
- X, Y, and Z are the sole multi-colour exception because their familiar mapping is functional.
- Axis letters, shape, status text, and focus all duplicate colour meaning.
- Controls are compact but pointer and touch hit regions remain at least forty pixels.
- Corners are quiet and consistent: small controls use the medium radius; major panels stay square.
- Disabled tools remain legible at tertiary contrast and always explain the connected/paused gate.
- Mono type is reserved for coordinates, IDs, snap values, shortcuts, and machine history.
- Responsive layouts protect the viewport first and move secondary docks into drawers or sheets.
- Logical inline/block properties carry layout so RTL does not break the editor.
- The system never uses gradients, neon decoration, glossy chrome, or invented product content.
